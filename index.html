Vitamin D3 and K2: A Simple Guide for Adults Over 50

Vitamin D is one of the nutrients people commonly talk about after 50, because the body makes less of it from sunlight as we age. Spending a little time outdoors, eating foods such as fortified milk, eggs, and oily fish, and talking with a healthcare professional about your own needs are sensible everyday steps. Vitamin K2 is a related nutrient found in fermented foods and some animal products, and it often comes up in conversations about balanced nutrition.
